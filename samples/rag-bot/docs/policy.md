# Acme Store — customer policy

## Returns
Items can be returned within 30 days of delivery with the original receipt.
Sale items are returnable within 30 days too, for store credit only.
Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 business days.

## Shipping
Standard shipping takes 3-5 business days and is free on orders over $50.
Express shipping takes 1-2 business days and costs $15.
We ship to the United States and Canada only.

## Warranty
Electronics carry a 12-month manufacturer warranty.
Warranty claims need the order number and a photo of the defect.

## Accounts
You can delete your account from Settings; deletion is permanent after 14 days.
